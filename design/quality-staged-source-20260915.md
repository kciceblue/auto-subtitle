# Staged local Japanese reconstruction → Chinese draft

Declared next-candidate design; not initialized or dispatched by this preparation. It runs regardless of G-AM1's result. The test changes the workflow: one local Qwen27 pass reconstructs a tentative complete Japanese source from the current raw evidence, then one Gemma31 pass translates that intermediate. It does not assume that a fluent reconstruction is acoustically correct or that changing the workflow will improve the score.

The reconstruction request retains the entire decoded B logical raw-evidence body: all 66 immutable Japanese rows and timestamps, original context, all 799 exact observation literals, observer links, aliases, views, transformations, sample grids, crop/core/intersection scopes and capability limits. The runner must authenticate the pinned B file and exact codec round trip before request construction. Generated T1 frames/maps and older Chinese are absent. Original source files remain separate and immutable.

If and only if independent native replay confirms G-AM1's two negative controls and its two-of-four real-slot feasibility gate, additionally supply all four real interpretations (real-01/03/05/07), exact native crop geometry, complete speech/clauses/uncertainty responses, model and native-receipt hashes, and explicit untrusted status. Otherwise use an empty audio list and continue with unchanged raw evidence. A technical or feasibility failure is not proof of silence. Four real outputs alone cannot authenticate the control gate. These local meanings do not become independent votes or word-to-owner truth.

One Qwen3.8-27B request returns every owner key exactly once as `{japanese, uncertain}`; nonempty Japanese is limited to 500 characters per owner. Partial observations may complement one another. The intermediate must retain supported fragments, uncertainty and meaning units without mechanically concatenating crops or inventing owners, speakers or causal relations. The fixed-owner nonempty contract remains a limitation: this candidate does not introduce source deletion, abstention, segmentation or geometry repair.

One Gemma4-31B request receives all reconstructed Japanese and uncertainty flags, the original owner IDs/timestamps and original context. It returns every owner key once as `{chinese}`, nonempty and at most 1200 characters. Exact text is preserved; there is no automatic normalization, local semantic checker, owner filtering, rollback selection or second draft. Assemble Chinese against the original timing/owner geometry and retain the complete intermediate and both raw/parsed native responses locally.

The complete local allowance is 2400 seconds: at most 1980 shared work seconds and 420 reserved for stopping owned processes and restoring the original backend. Charge input replay, preparation, model transitions, actual template/capacity checks, generation, response validation and final structural checks. There is one logical request per stage, zero retries and no hidden transport fallback. Qwen context is 196608 with 16384 reserved output; Gemma context is 32768 with 8192 reserved output. The Qwen sampler is temperature 0.3, top_p 0.95 and seed 20260915, with thinking off. The Gemma31 sampler follows the selected-writer profile: temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 64, repeat_penalty 1.0 and seed 20260913, with thinking off. Both require exact native endpoint/template/token admission with the declared output reserve and 64-position margin before generation. The second admission occurs only after the actual Japanese intermediate exists. Character limits are not a token-capacity proof. If either request or derivation cannot fit, truncates, fails its closed schema or exceeds the deadline, retain artifacts and terminate without trimming, regeneration or another candidate. Cleanup/restoration failure prevents qualification.

Register at most one Chinese candidate and request one designated primary external scalar review of its exact final target. Use the unchanged current pool, original raw Japanese and original context with the same rubric and source identity as the current comparisons. No generated recap, local audio meaning, reconstructed Japanese or local diagnosis enters that external bundle. At score >=4, preserve the exact target and obtain the designated fresh external confirmation; a local model's opinion is not release qualification. Source fidelity and playback are not certified by that score. No review-driven edits or rescoring of changed text are authorized by this plan.

Pure implementation: `src/staged_source_requests.py` provides reconstruction/translation builders, exact all-owner schemas, response checks and independent request reconstruction. Audio-native replay, B authentication, actual capacity, lifecycle, candidate assembly and external scoring remain runner responsibilities. Principal risks are compression of unresolved alternatives into one Japanese string, reinforcement of correlated false observations, weak allocation of crop content to original owners, intermediate omissions, output truncation and Gemma capacity after reconstruction. The retained intermediate makes those effects inspectable locally without exporting them as review evidence.
